<a id="vazirmatn"></a>
## [↑](#row-vazirmatn) Vazirmatn

**Description:** A simple and highly legible Persian/Arabic sans-serif typeface designed for web and applications. Started in 2015 as Vazir and later expanded; Latin glyphs are taken from Roboto.

**Designer:** Saber Rastikerdar

**Style:** Sans

**License:** OFL

**Languages:** Arabic, Kurdish (Sorani), Persian, English, Pashto, Uyghur, Urdu

**Weights:** Variable

**Note:** Extended successor of the Vazir font

[Source](https://github.com/rastikerdar/vazirmatn) - [Download](https://github.com/alr-rashidi/Awesome-Persian-Fonts/raw/refs/heads/main/data/font-files/vazirmatn-v33.003.zip)

## [↑](#row-aradvf) Arad

**Description:** A geometric Persian-Arabic sans-serif, the modern successor of Shabnam. Available in 10 weights and multiple dot styles, suitable for both body text and display.

**Designer:** Mohammad Darvishi

**Style:** Sans

**License:** OFL

**Languages:** Arabic, Kurdish (Sorani), Persian, English, Urdu

**Weights:** Variable

**Variable Axes:** `DSTY` (Dots Style) : 1–3

[Source](https://github.com/MohamadDarvishi/Arad) - [Download](https://github.com/alr-rashidi/Awesome-Persian-Fonts/raw/refs/heads/main/data/font-files/Arad_2.5.0.zip)
